Choose Your Adventure

Easy

Jungle Float

A peaceful 2-hour journey through lush rainforest. Perfect for families, first-timers, and anyone who wants to soak in the scenery without the adrenaline spike.

  • Duration: 2 hours
  • Min. Age: 6 years
  • Group Size: Up to 12
  • Price: $45 per person
Moderate

Rapids Rush

Half a day of Class II–III rapids with splashing waves and fast water. Our most popular trip — a genuine rush without requiring prior experience.

  • Duration: 4 hours
  • Min. Age: 12 years
  • Group Size: Up to 8
  • Price: $75 per person
Extreme

Yaque Xtreme

A full-day assault on Class III–IV rapids through the canyon stretch of Río Yaque del Norte. For experienced paddlers who want the real thing — no shortcuts, no guardrails.

  • Duration: Full day (8 hrs)
  • Min. Age: 16 years
  • Group Size: Up to 6
  • Price: $120 per person
